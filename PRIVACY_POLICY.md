### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **100000+ Hindi GK Questions** ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and demonstrates our dedication to keeping your personal data completely secure.

### 2. Zero Personal Data Collection
Our application operates **100% offline**. 
- We do **NOT** require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, or any other personal details.
- We do **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to any third party or remote server.

### 3. Local Data Storage & Preferences
All data created during your use of the App is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (via local SQLite database and secure local preferences):
- **Quiz Scores & Statistics:** Your scores, completed levels, and progress.
- **Bookmarks & Saved Questions:** Any questions you mark for revision.
- **Daily Goals & Streaks:** Study tracker and streak milestones.
- **App Settings:** Your preferences such as Dark/Light theme, audio effects, and Text-to-Speech (TTS) speeds.

This data never leaves your device. You can completely erase this data at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the application.

### 4. Device Permissions
The App is designed with privacy-first principles and requires **no sensitive device permissions**:
- No Camera access
- No Location / GPS access
- No Contacts access
- No Microphone access
- No SMS or Phone access

### 5. Third-Party Services & Analytics
- We do **NOT** use any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies that monitor your activity.
- The app does not ping any remote servers during normal quiz gameplay or study sessions.

### 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)
Our App is safe for all age groups, including students and children preparing for competitive examinations or academic quizzes. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Since the App operates offline without account registration, any updates will be posted directly to this page or included in future app releases.
